Our quick-check buying guide.

01 / VALUE

Compare the total cost

Check delivery charges, accessories, replacement parts and subscriptions. A cheaper initial price does not always mean better long-term value.

02 / FIT

Check the details

Measure your space, verify UK plug and voltage requirements where relevant, and compare dimensions with your existing items.

03 / CONFIDENCE

Read beyond the stars

Check recent customer feedback for recurring issues, look for warranty and returns information, and confirm seller details before purchasing.
